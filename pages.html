The Land Far From Home

Ballpoint Pages · 2026

School, is it a place where we come to learn life or where we come to socialize? In the end we do both before we realize. Betrayals of all sorts Chores in and out of dorms Friends, when you make Hold them well before they come at stake. Circumstance play a bigger role Then before you know it you are the lonely pole Goodness doesn't yield much Evil doesn't, as a matter of fact. — BlackScribe
